How to connect your domain

  1. Add your domain to Cloudflare and point its nameservers there.
  2. Go to Zero Trust → Networks → Tunnels → click your tunnel.
  3. Open the Public Hostnames tab → Add a public hostname.
  4. Set Domain to your domain, Service to HTTP, URL to localhost:80.
  5. Save — Cloudflare provisions SSL automatically.
