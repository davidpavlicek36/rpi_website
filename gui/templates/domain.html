DNS setup guide

  1. Find your Pi's public IP: curl -s ifconfig.me
  2. In your domain registrar / DNS provider, add an A record:
    @ → <your public IP>
  3. Wait for DNS to propagate (usually 1–15 minutes, up to 24 h).
  4. Save domain above, then click Get Free SSL Certificate.

No static IP? Use a DDNS service like DuckDNS or Cloudflare.
